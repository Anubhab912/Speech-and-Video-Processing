## Module Overview & Syllabus Breakdown (8 Lectures)

### Topic 1: Optical Flow & Motion Representation
- Optical flow constraint equation
- Apparent motion vs. true 3D motion fields

### Topic 2: Motion Estimation Criteria & Optimization
- Matching criteria: MAD, MSE, SAD
- Optimization techniques for motion search

### Topic 3: Pixel-Based & Block-Matching Motion Estimation
- Exhaustive Block Matching Algorithm (EBMA)
- Fast search algorithms (Three-Step Search, 2D Logarithmic Search, Diamond Search)

### Topic 4: Gradient-Based & Feature-Based Methods
- Horn-Schunck and Lucas-Kanade optical flow formulations
- Intensity matching vs. keypoint feature matching (SIFT, ORB)

### Topic 5: Frequency-Domain Motion Estimation & Depth
- Phase correlation and frequency-domain velocity estimation
- Depth recovery from optical flow fields

### Topic 6: Video Summarization & Surveillance Applications
- Keyframe extraction and video summarization
- Automated video surveillance and anomaly detection
