## Module Overview & Syllabus Breakdown (7 Lectures | Serials 34–40)

### Topic 1: 2D/3D Video Tracking & Contour Tracking
- Bounding box and blob tracking methods
- Kernel-based tracking (Mean-Shift, CamShift)
- Active contours and snake models

### Topic 2: Filtering, Mosaicking, and Video Segmentation
- Kalman filtering and Particle filtering for state tracking
- Video mosaicking and panoramic stitching
- Mean-shift video segmentation algorithms

### Topic 3: Active Shape Models, Shot Boundary Detection & Compression
- Active Shape Models (ASM) and Active Appearance Models (AAM)
- Video shot boundary detection (abrupt cuts vs. gradual transitions)
- Inter-frame motion compensation and video compression standards (MPEG/H.264)

### Topic 4: Python & OpenCV Implementation
- Frame extraction, color space conversion (`cv2.cvtColor`)
- Mean-shift and CamShift object tracking implementation using OpenCV
