## Module Overview & Syllabus Breakdown (8 Lectures | Serials 18–25)

### Topic 1: Video Formation, Perception, and Representation
- Video capture principles, frame rates, and temporal perception
- Analog and digital video formats

### Topic 2: Color Video Principles & Camera Models
- Color spaces (RGB, YUV, YCbCr)
- Video camera operation, sensors (CCD/CMOS), and displays
- Pinhole camera geometry and perspective projection

### Topic 3: Camera Motion, Shape, and Scene Models
- Camera pan, tilt, zoom, and translation modeling
- 2D/3D shape representations and scene modeling

### Topic 4: Motion Models & Projective Mapping
- 2D Motion Models (Affine, Bilinear, Projective)
- 3D Rigid Motion and Projective Mapping transformations
